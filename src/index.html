    The Fringe Cube

    Play The Fringe Cube directly in your browser. Solve puzzles, test your reflexes, and experience interactive web gameplay online for free.

    Game Instructions & Controls

    Navigate the 3D spatial hypergrid using intuitive mouse or touch controls:

    • Mouse Controls: Left-click on any empty cell to place your mark. Click and drag in open space to rotate the 3D cube. Use the mouse scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
    • Touch Controls: Tap empty cells to place moves. Drag to rotate the 3D cube and pinch to zoom on touch devices.
    • View Switching: Toggle between the fully 3D rotatable cube (3D Spatial) and flattened layer slices (2D Planes) for tactical planning.
    • Layer Isolation: Use layer isolation buttons (L1, L2, L3, All) and the explode slider to inspect interior cells.

    Gameplay Rules & Win Conditions

    The Fringe Cube expands classic tic-tac-toe into a 3×3×3 three-dimensional matrix with 27 cells across three horizontal layers (Bottom, Middle, Top).

    • Players take turns placing their marker (✕, ◯, or △).
    • There are 49 possible winning lines: 27 axial rows/columns across the X, Y, and Z axes, 18 layer diagonals, and 4 cross-cube space diagonals.
    • The first player to align 3 consecutive marks along any of the 49 vectors wins the game.
    • Play against tactical computer AI (Easy, Medium, or Hard), enjoy local 2-Player matches, or engage in 3-Player multi-battles.

    3D TIC-TAC-TOE FIELD GUIDE

    Complete Rules, 49 Winning Lines, Spatial Controls & Winning Strategies
    ◈ What is The Fringe Cube?

    The Fringe Cube is a spatial evolution of the ancient game of Tic-Tac-Toe. Instead of playing on a flat 3×3 square with 9 cells, the battlefield expands into a full 3×3×3 matrix composed of 27 distinct cells across 3 vertical layers:

    LAYER 1 (L1) BOTTOM
    Cells 0 through 8. The foundational ground plane of the matrix.
    LAYER 2 (L2) EQUATOR / CORE
    Cells 9 through 17. Contains cell #13, the absolute gravitational center of the cube.
    LAYER 3 (L3) TOP SKY
    Cells 18 through 26. The upper celestial layer overlooking the matrix.
    ⚖ Core Objective & Turn Flow
    • The Win Condition: Be the first player to align 3 identical marks in a straight continuous line in 3D space.
    • Turn Order: Players take turns placing their unique symbol into any empty cell.
    • Player ✕ (Cyan): Always moves first (Round opener).
    • Player ◯ (Pink): Moves second (In vs AI, played by the computer).
    • Player △ (Neon Green): Moves third when playing in 3-Player (3P) game modes.
    • Stalemate / Draw: If all 27 cells are occupied without any 3-in-a-row alignment, the game ends in a draw.
    📐 The Geometry of 49 Winning Lines

    In standard 2D tic-tac-toe, there are only 8 winning lines. In 3D space, the combination of 3 orthogonal axes and spatial diagonals yields exactly 49 unique winning lines:

    27 ORTHOGONAL LINES 1D AXIAL
    • 9 Rows (X-axis): 3 horizontal rows across each of the 3 layers.
    • 9 Columns (Z-axis): 3 depth columns across each layer.
    • 9 Pillars (Y-axis): 9 vertical elevators connecting identical (X, Z) positions straight up through L1 → L2 → L3.
    18 PLANAR DIAGONALS 2D SLICES
    • 6 Horizontal Diagonals: 2 classic diagonal X-crosses inside each horizontal plane (L1, L2, L3).
    • 6 Vertical Front-to-Back: 2 diagonals on each vertical slice (X=0, 1, 2).
    • 6 Vertical Left-to-Right: 2 diagonals on each cross slice (Z=0, 1, 2).
    4 TRUE SPACE DIAGONALS 3D HYPER-CROSS
    The four most lethal vectors in the game! They connect opposite corners of the 3D cube and all pass directly through the absolute center cell (1, 1, 1):
    1. Bottom-Front-Left [0,0,0] → Center [1,1,1] → Top-Back-Right [2,2,2]
    2. Bottom-Front-Right [2,0,0] → Center [1,1,1] → Top-Back-Left [0,2,2]
    3. Bottom-Back-Left [0,0,2] → Center [1,1,1] → Top-Front-Right [2,2,0]
    4. Bottom-Back-Right [2,0,2] → Center [1,1,1] → Top-Front-Left [0,2,0]

    💥 Explode Layers & Layer Isolation

    Because internal cells can be occluded by outer cells, The Fringe Cube provides specialized spatial tools:

    EXPLODE LAYERS SLIDER VERTICAL EXPANSION
    Slide to increase vertical distance between L1, L2, and L3. Allows clear sightlines directly into the center core of the cube.
    ISOLATE LAYER (L1, L2, L3, ALL) FILTER VIEW
    Isolates a single horizontal plane to examine local 2D threats without background visual clutter. Select ALL to restore full cube visibility.
    2D PLANES MODE FLAT PROJECTION
    Switch from "3D SPATIAL" to "2D PLANES" to unfold the cube into three parallel 3×3 flat boards. Hovering over any cell automatically highlights its corresponding vertical pillar neighbors across all 3 planes!
    ⚡ Secret 1: The Core Nexus (Cell #13)

    In standard 2D Tic-Tac-Toe, the center square belongs to 4 lines. In 3D Tic-Tac-Toe, Cell 13 (Layer 2, Row 2, Col 2) belongs to an astonishing 13 WINNING LINES!

    It is part of: 3 Orthogonal Lines (1 pillar, 1 row, 1 col) + 6 Planar Diagonals + all 4 Space Diagonals! Controlling the center cell gives you unmatched offensive flexibility and defensive denial.

    👑 Secret 2: Corner Domination (8 Anchors)

    The 8 corner cells are part of 7 winning lines each (3 orthogonal lines, 3 planar diagonals, and 1 space diagonal). Playing corners early allows you to anchor cross-layer diagonals that opponents frequently overlook.

    🔱 Secret 3: Constructing 3D Forks

    A "Fork" occurs when one of your moves creates two simultaneous 2-in-a-row winning threats on different vectors. Because your opponent can only block one line on their turn, you are guaranteed victory on your following move. Look for forks that combine a vertical pillar with a planar diagonal!

    ⚠️ Secret 4: Beware 3-Player Spoilers

    In 3-Player mode, blind blocking can be fatal. If Player 1 has two marks in a row and Player 2 blocks them, Player 3 might capitalize on an open vector. Always calculate whose turn comes next before placing a non-offensive mark.
